Privacy Policy

Effective June 7, 2026. Developer: Hobart Price.

Actuator Control stores actuator preferences, the selected theme, custom button labels, the actuator identifier and local address, safety acknowledgment, and demonstration-mode preference on the iPhone. The pairing key is stored in the iOS Keychain.

Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode unlocks protected controls on that iPhone. After pairing, one successful check lasts until the iPhone is turned off. The app does not receive, store, or transmit biometric information.

The app uses the local network and Bluetooth only to discover and control the local actuator. It does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, a cloud account, or a developer-operated data server.

Hobart Price does not receive, sell, rent, or share information processed by the app.

Local preferences remain until changed or deleted. The pairing key remains in Keychain until you delete local app data, forget the stored key, or remove the app. Delete Local App Data is under Settings, then Safety, privacy, and support.
