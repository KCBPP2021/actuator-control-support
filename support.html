Actuator Control Support

Actuator Control is a local iPhone controller for a compatible actuator. It does not use an account or a developer server.

Email bartandpaigekc@gmail.com with the app version, actuator firmware version, iPhone model, and a description of the problem.

Wi-Fi

Keep the iPhone and the actuator on the same non-guest Wi-Fi network. Allow Local Network access when iOS asks. On Home, tap Search Wi-Fi.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is a backup when Wi-Fi is unavailable. The first pairing still uses the six-digit PIN over Wi-Fi. After that, Face ID, Touch ID, or the iPhone passcode unlocks protected controls until you close the app or restart the iPhone. Switching away without closing the app does not ask again. Stop is never locked behind that check.

Practice without hardware

On Home, tap Try without hardware. The practice controls open immediately. Extend, Retract, and Stop only change the on-screen state. Nothing is sent to an actuator.

To leave practice, tap Exit practice on Home, or open Settings, Practice without hardware, and turn Practice mode off. Closing the app also turns practice off. Switching to another app without closing this one leaves practice on.

Delete data on this iPhone

Settings, then Safety, privacy, and support, then Delete Local App Data removes the saved actuator identity, address, and the Keychain pairing key from this iPhone. That key is removed even when the actuator is not connected.

Emergency

Do not troubleshoot a moving mechanism from inside its path. Use the physical emergency stop or disconnect power before inspecting hardware.
